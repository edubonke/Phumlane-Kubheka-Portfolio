About · Silicon, Systems & Students

Where hands-on infrastructure meets patient teaching.

Working across computer labs, live user environments, and modern browsers taught me a foundational truth: technology only succeeds when the people using it feel capable.

In Johannesburg, my work spans practical IT and systems support—maintaining computer labs, troubleshooting networks, resolving hardware and OS faults—alongside front-end web development and ICT lecturing. Supporting everyday learners and end-users sharpens my empathy as a developer, while building for the web keeps my classroom instruction current.

Alongside core technical delivery, my background in project management, data fundamentals, and artificial intelligence fundamentals lets me structure initiatives clearly, interpret operational patterns, and guide learners toward an AI-literate future.

Systematic Root-Cause Clarity

Whether isolating a workstation boot failure in a computer lab or debugging a layout state bug, I replace guesswork with calm, repeatable triage and preventative documentation.
